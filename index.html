Welcome to Central Oregon Woodworkers

Where craftsmanship meets creativity

A place for woodworkers, turners and carvers to meet and share knowledge and ideas. COW is run by volunteers and there are no dues or fees to participate in any meetings or workshops. We welcome everyone regardless of skill level. Beginners, Professional or those just curious about woodworking.

COW consists of 3 different groups, each with their own facilitator. Meetings are very casual and welcoming. Meetings may have a demonstration and/or a show and tell of recent projects followed by general discussion among participants.

Woodworkers

The Bend area group meets on the second Tuesday of the month.

The Sister area group meets on the first Tuesday of the month.

Everyone is welcome to attend any meetings regardless of your location.
