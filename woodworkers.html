Woodworkers

We are a small group of woodworkers and wood turners from Central Oregon (Bend, Redmond, Sisters, Sunriver, Lapine, Prineville and other towns in the area) of all ilks, from professionals to hobbyists.

In May 2018 we formed the Sisters Area Woodwokers (SAW) who hold meetings in their area on the first Tuesday of the month from 7-9 pm.

The Bend area group continues to meet once a month, usually at someone's shop, on the second Tuesday of the month from 7 - 9 pm.

Meeting date and times are listed on the COW Meeting schedule.

Both the Bend area and Sisters area meetings are open to all who are interested in wood and there is no charge to belong to either or both groups.
