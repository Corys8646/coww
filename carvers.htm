Carvers

We are a small group of wood carvers from Central Oregon (Bend, Redmond, Sisters, Sunriver, Lapine, Prineville and other towns in the area). We started holding monthly meetings in January 2015 and hope to offer classes for new and continuing carvers in the area. Meeting date and times are listed on the COW Meeting schedule.

If you have questions concerning carving or would like to know more please contact Scott Beyer at Scott@centraloregonwoodworkers.net
