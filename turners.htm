Turners

We are a small group of wood turners from Central Oregon (Bend, Redmond, Sisters, Sunriver, Lapine, Prineville and other towns in the area) of all skill levels, from professionals to hobbyists. We meet once a quarter, usually at Ken's shop where we have three lathes available for demonstration and practice. There is no charge to belong to the group. Meeting date and times are listed on the COW Meeting schedule.

Members of the group would be happy to talk to you about your turning needs, be it bowls, vases, stair or chair spindles, dough rollers, bird houses or what have you.

Email Chris Matthews to join the google group.
